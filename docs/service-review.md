# Batangas City service applicability and location review

Source: [City Government of Batangas Citizen’s Charter 2026 (2nd Edition)](https://www.batangascity.gov.ph/web/images/downloads/City-Government-of-Batangas-Citizens-Charter-2026-2nd-Edition.pdf) supplied by the user. Reviewed 11 November 2026. The PDF has 1,133 pages; printed and PDF page numbers match. Its SHA-256 is `b9688a7329475428048adedab9482da62d103790e8d19ccc84cd7ff7ee31fdce`.

## Coverage and interpretation

All **57 search-indexed records** are mapped: **32 documented city services, 20 applicable with corrected scope, 3 barangay/external referrals and 2 not established by this Charter**. Nine additional category-card services are reviewed separately (six confirmed, one scope correction, two not established). Twenty-two existing service-detail pages are linked to 21 curated office-directory records. The original service/category headings were inventoried so duplicate and embedded legacy claims could be identified. This is not an exhaustive new catalogue of every transaction in the 1,133-page Charter.

“Documented” verifies the existence and office/scope of the cited transaction, not a guarantee of today’s schedule, fees, funding or live portal availability. “Not established” is an evidence gap, not a claim that the service does not exist. All classifications below distinguish city provision from city assistance with another agency’s service.

## Implemented in this pass

- A source-cited service map and office-location directory, with page links into the official PDF.
- Category listings generated from the reviewed records; unsupported generic programs and unconfigured payments are no longer presented as ready-to-use services. Hospital phone numbers link to the existing Contact directory.
- Corrected service-search titles, provider routing, descriptions and online/reference destinations while retaining existing IDs and category links.
- Verified address references on all 22 detail pages and replacement of the inherited location blocks. Office locations are distinguished from clinical, inspection, field-service and operating-site locations.
- Removed municipal-office labels from relevant visible service-office names; retained existing filenames so links continue to work.
- EN/FIL labels and address values; official source-derived names, summaries and citations retain their English wording.
- Inherited process tables have been replaced with source-backed summaries on all 22 detail pages. Page-specific Charter links provide the full official checklist, fees and timeline; these tables have not been independently transcribed into the portal. No bank accounts, staff names, invented floors, coordinates, service hours or benefit guarantees were imported.

## Embedded detail-page findings and treatment

The old procedural text has been removed from the public guides. These grouped findings explain the replacement: applicable transactions link to their official Charter entries, duplicate routes are retained for compatibility, and unsupported/internal legacy claims are not promoted as public entitlements. Detailed local checklists, fees and processing tables can be transcribed in a subsequent evidence review; this pass provides the full primary-source links.
