“Documented” records what the cited Charter establishes. It does not guarantee current availability, office hours, funding or live online transactions. “Not established” is an evidence gap, not proof that a service does not exist.

## Verification before relying on a record

1. Read the cited transaction and its complete checklist, fee table, processing-time table and applicant eligibility. Confirm that the interpretation matches the specific service, not only an office’s general mandate.
2. Check for later official notices or amendments. Record the source, date, page and any correction in the mapping files.
3. Distinguish the administrative office address from the actual receiving counter, clinic, inspection venue, field-service location or operating facility.
4. Resolve the flagged gaps: OSCA’s building/street, a separate OSCP receiving location if applicable, clinic-specific venues, the slaughterhouse operating-site address and unconfirmed programs/payment channels. The Engineer’s street spelling and duplicated veterinary office-directory label also need corroboration.
5. Verify any online application or payment destination through the official city channel before describing it as available. This reference does not authorize copying payment account details or promising benefits.

The proposed website uses source-backed summaries and links to the full official transactions. It does not independently reproduce or verify every requirements, fees and processing-time table. Keep unresolved details explicit until they are verified.
